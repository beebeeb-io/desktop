Task 1683 · two-surface redesign · design for review · 24 Oct 2026

Two surfaces: the popover under the b, and one Beebeeb window

Today a Mac can show four Beebeeb windows at once. After this change there are two places, and only two: the small popover that opens under the b in the menu bar, and one Beebeeb window. That window holds sign-in, setting up this Mac, Home, Review, Trash and Settings. The gear navigates to Settings inside it; nothing opens a separate window.

“we already created an artifact, but how the signin is right now and that we have multiple screens doesnt make sense. So i want max 2 screens, the little one below the B in the topbar and just the app itself where you get signin, etc etc. Including settings. The gear menu described in the side menu should just navigate to the bigger screen settings and should look the same as macos apps regularly look like.”Guus, 24 Oct 2026
“I want it more like how proton looks. Now also our version is not nicely aligned, not small, has too much info etc. Also when i just start beebeeb that window is randomly in the screen and not at the top bar. Often see too many windows going around.”Guus, 30 Sep 2026

Spec: docs/specs/2026-10-24-macos-two-surface-app.md. Every current window, page, setting and dialog is mapped there to its new home, or listed as removed with the reason: 117 found, 78 moved, 11 kept as they are, 24 removed, 4 Windows-only. Use the button at the top right to switch light and dark; section 7 shows both themes side by side.

1 · The popover, state by state

Fixed at 372 × 488 pt (unchanged from 30 Sep), so it never jumps between states. Header: account and storage. Body: what is happening, or one message with one action. Footer: the same three actions every time. Counts, names and sizes are examples.

2 · Signed out: the same window holds sign-in and setting up this Mac

No sidebar while there is no session: one centred card, as spec C approved, in the window that later shows Home. The window title follows the screen (“Sign in to Beebeeb”). After sign-in the card becomes the next step in place; no new window opens and nothing closes by itself. Spec C’s removals stand: no five-step rail, no “Choose offline folders”, no “Control center is ready”.

3 · Signed in: Home, Review, Trash

Three places in the sidebar and the gear. Files live in Finder, so the window does not have a second file browser: the Windows shell’s “Files” page was a sync summary, and its numbers move to Home. Account, Devices, Security, Insights and Bandwidth move into Settings (section 4) or are listed as removed in the spec.

4 · Settings, inside the window

The gear at the bottom of the sidebar, ⌘, and the popover’s “Settings…” all land here. The content area becomes a standard macOS Settings pane: icon tabs in the toolbar, grouped inset rows, labels left and controls right, the look approved on 30 Sep. The sidebar stays, with the gear marked as current. The four shipped tabs keep their rows and copy; Security is new and collects what the Windows shell had in Account, Devices, Security and Activity.

5 · Sign out (task 1887), now in the Beebeeb window

The flow moves with Settings › Account and keeps every word. Each sheet is window-modal: it covers the sidebar too, so you cannot navigate away mid-question. Closing the window ends the flow exactly as closing Settings did: closed while sending means closed, closed at the question means Cancel. Counts and paths are examples.

8 · Three questions for you

Everything else is decided in the spec, with the reason. A one-word answer per number is enough.

  1. 1

    How Settings looks

    RecommendedIcon tabs in the window’s toolbar (as drawn): the Settings look you approved on 30 Sep, now inside the main window.

    Or System Settings style: the categories replace the sidebar while you are in Settings, with a back arrow.

  2. 2

    Windows-shell pages that don’t come to the Mac

    RecommendedLeave out the 20-hour traffic chart and live session list (Bandwidth), App Activity (CPU, memory, PID), the account notifications list, and the switches a Mac never honours (metered, Files on Demand, File Explorer overlays). Each is listed in the spec with its reason.

    Or keep any of them, in Settings.

  3. 3

    The app menu’s “Sign Out…”

    RecommendedAfter task 1887’s device check passes, make it open the same “Sign out of this Mac?” sheet as Settings, so there is one way to sign out.

    Today it signs out at once when nothing is waiting. This redesign keeps that until you decide.

9 · What is not checked yet

  • These are browser drawings of macOS chrome (menu bar, traffic lights, native menu and alert). Nothing in this page has run on a Mac.
  • Emails, names, sizes, codes, paths and counts are invented examples. The recovery words are not a real phrase.
  • Whether the Windows shell’s Mac-visible pages work against a Mac’s engine (the file overview counts on Home, Free up space, the File menu’s Upload and New folder) is not determined from the code; the spec names each one.
  • Why the Windows shell window showed on the lead’s Mac is not confirmed: the likeliest cause, read from the code, is the shared config creating main-app on every platform and the window-state plugin restoring it as visible.
